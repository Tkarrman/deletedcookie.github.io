Production Timeline

Production Milestones

The updated roadmap runs from the current friends-and-family playable through the July 2027 full release.

1. Playable Build & Friends-and-Family Playtest 2. Public Reveal 3. Closed Public Playtest 4. Open Demo Testing 5. Steam Next Fest 6. Early Access Release 7. Early Access Updates & 1.0 Preparation 8. 1.0 Full Release
Sep 2026 · Now
Milestone 1

Playable Build & Friends-and-Family Playtest

A playable build is being tested internally with friends and family to check the core co-op loop and identify the biggest issues.

Goal

Establish a fun, repeatable multiplayer session before inviting a wider audience.

Focus

Core loop clarity, controls, treatment gameplay, co-op communication, and stability.

Key Deliverables
  • Playable internal build
  • Friends-and-family test sessions
  • Collected feedback and prioritized fixes
Validation

Testers can complete a session together and describe what was fun, confusing, or broken.

Outcome

A tested playable foundation and a clear list of improvements for the reveal.

Late Sep 2026
Milestone 2

Public Reveal

Reveal Plague Crew at the end of September with a clear pitch, gameplay footage, and a place for interested players to follow the game.

Goal

Introduce the game publicly and begin building awareness ahead of external testing.

Focus

Reveal messaging, gameplay capture, trailer or teaser, Steam presence, and community channels.

Key Deliverables
  • Reveal announcement and gameplay footage
  • Updated Steam page and wishlist call to action
  • Community channels and testing sign-up
Validation

The reveal clearly communicates the co-op premise and gives players a way to follow or join future tests.

Outcome

A public identity for the game and an initial pool of interested testers.

Oct 2026
Milestone 3

Closed Public Playtest

Invite a limited group of players outside the team to test the game and provide structured feedback.

Goal

Learn how new players experience the game without guidance from the developers.

Focus

Onboarding, multiplayer reliability, pacing, balance, and the most confusing parts of the loop.

Key Deliverables
  • Access-controlled test build
  • Tester invitations and feedback form
  • Bug triage and playtest report
Validation

External groups can start, play, and finish sessions; their feedback identifies the highest-priority changes.

Outcome

A better-informed plan for the open demo and a more stable build.

Dec 2026–Jan 2027
Milestone 4

Open Demo Testing

Make the demo available for open testing through December and January, then use player feedback to improve it.

Goal

Test the demo with a broader audience before Steam Next Fest.

Focus

Demo onboarding, performance, co-op stability, replayability, and public feedback.

Key Deliverables
  • Public demo build
  • Feedback and crash-reporting process
  • Demo updates informed by player sessions
Validation

New players can enjoy the demo without developer assistance, and the main technical issues are tracked and addressed.

Outcome

A tested, improved demo ready for the February showcase.

Feb 2027
Milestone 5

Steam Next Fest

Show the refined demo during the planned February Steam Next Fest window and turn interest into wishlists and community growth.

Goal

Put the strongest version of the demo in front of a larger Steam audience.

Focus

Demo polish, store presentation, creator outreach, support, and rapid issue response.

Key Deliverables
  • Next Fest demo build
  • Updated store and promotional materials
  • Player support and post-event feedback review
Validation

The demo runs reliably for new players and the team can respond quickly to reported issues.

Outcome

Broader player awareness and a prioritized final pass for Early Access.

Mar 2027
Milestone 6

Early Access Release

Launch Plague Crew in Early Access with a stable core experience and a clear plan for updates toward 1.0.

Goal

Release a playable, enjoyable game that can grow through player feedback.

Focus

Launch stability, onboarding, balancing, community support, and update planning.

Key Deliverables
  • Early Access release build
  • Store and launch materials
  • Known-issues list and update roadmap
Validation

Players can reliably play the core co-op experience and understand what is coming next.

Outcome

A public Early Access launch and an active feedback loop with players.

Apr–Jun 2027
Milestone 7

Early Access Updates & 1.0 Preparation

Use the months after Early Access to improve the game, expand content, and prepare the full release.

Goal

Turn player feedback into a more complete and polished 1.0 experience.

Focus

Progression, villages, events, treatments, co-op balance, accessibility, performance, and release preparation.

Key Deliverables
  • Regular Early Access fixes and updates
  • Expanded content and progression
  • Performance, accessibility, and onboarding passes
  • 1.0 release candidate and launch materials
Validation

The game has enough depth and polish for a full release, with major player issues resolved.

Outcome

A release candidate ready for final verification and July launch.

Jul 2027
Milestone 8

1.0 Full Release

Launch the complete 1.0 version in July with the final content, polish, and release campaign.

Goal

Deliver the full Plague Crew experience to players.

Focus

Final quality checks, launch readiness, store materials, press and creator outreach, and player support.

Key Deliverables
  • 1.0 release build
  • Final trailer and store updates
  • Launch communications and support plan
Validation

The release build meets the team's quality bar and is ready for a broad audience.

Outcome

Plague Crew 1.0 launches in July 2027.
