01 / About

Technology in service of play.

I work where engineering, design, and production meet: shaping systems that feel good to play, are practical to build, and remain healthy through production.

I’m a game developer and technical leader with more than 14 years of experience developing and shipping games across PC, consoles, and mobile.

I’ve co-founded multiple game studios and worked as Technical Director at Thunderful / The Station, contributing to titles including SteamWorld Build and ISLANDERS: New Shores.

Today I’m a co-founder of Deleted Cookie AB, where we develop original games and provide technical development and consulting services.

Based in Karlshamn, Sweden

Available for hands-on development, technical direction, architecture, prototyping, performance work, and embedded team support.
