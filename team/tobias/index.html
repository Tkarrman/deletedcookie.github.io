Tobias Kärrman

Technical Director & Game Developer

I love making games and figuring out what makes them feel alive. I’ve worked with Unity and C# since 2012, shipping games on mobile, PC, and consoles. My favourite part is seeing simple rules create surprising behaviours and building tools that help the team try out ideas.

Work Experience

Technical Director · Thunderful / The Station

2020–2025

I led technical work on SteamWorld Build and ISLANDERS: New Shores, both developed in Unity for PC, PlayStation, Xbox, and Nintendo Switch.

  • Built agent and enemy behaviours for the city and mines in SteamWorld Build. I worked on the simplified action-planning system and the behaviours that used it to choose what to do in each situation.
  • Built tools for Design and Art and helped establish workflows for asset creation, importing, and in-game configuration. One example let designers configure data in Google Sheets and import it directly into the game.
  • Worked with Programming, Design, Art, QA, production, and management to spot technical problems early. I coordinated with QA and helped turn creative goals into practical technical solutions.
  • Coordinated with the external studio that ported SteamWorld Build to Nintendo Switch. We tested on development hardware throughout production and set performance budgets for lower-spec platforms.
